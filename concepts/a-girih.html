Cyrus

Every company runs on data scattered across systems that disagree on what a customer, a batch or a contract is. Cyrus gives that data one shared meaning: an ontology of the objects a business runs on, so people and AI agents can ask questions in the language of the business and trace every answer back to its source. Built for any industry, first modelled on pharma, in Arabic and English.

Enterprise governance and identity

زاگرس

Zagros

Who is allowed to do what, and who decided? Zagros is one self-hosted system for authentication, authorization and governance, with no outside identity provider in the loop. An instance is founded in a sealed ceremony where three of five constitutional keys must agree, and every decision after that runs through policy.

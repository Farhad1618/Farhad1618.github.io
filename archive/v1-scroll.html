How I build

Three layers, always
Above — Governance

Every system held to what it is permitted to know.

Policy-based authorization, attribute-based access control, hash-chained audit trails. Agents and users act inside boundaries the system enforces rather than assumes.

The surface — Systems

APIs, agents, and products people actually touch.

Autonomous services that plan, call tools, and act against the platform's own APIs. Typed SDKs, passwordless identity, and infrastructure that scales from day one.

Beneath — Ontology

A graph, not a pile of rows.

A domain ontology and the pipelines built on it — the semantic layer every service and agent reads from. Relations are first-class, so reasoning is possible at all.

SR-71 Blackbird

Identity, authorization & governance platform · Apache 2.0

Multi-tenant identity and governance with no external identity provider in the loop. Passkeys, email OTP and passwords are all verified in-instance. Authorization runs on Cerbos policies, and the instance is brought up through a sealed Genesis ceremony with K-of-N constitutional keys and a catastrophe policy. FastAPI, Next.js and PostgreSQL, with TypeScript and Python SDKs — the same code runs local, on-premise, air-gapped or managed.

Most systems remember. I build systems that understand.

An ontology and a graph beneath the surface, so an AI reasons across relations rather than retrieving rows. A governance layer above, holding every system to what it is permitted to know. I take the hardest problems on purpose.

I am Chief Technology Officer at OnePharma in Dubai, where I designed the healthcare domain ontology and the data pipelines built on it — the semantic layer every service and agent reads from. I built the agentic AI layer: autonomous services that plan, call tools, and act against the platform's own APIs. I own the governance layer covering policy, permissions and audit, so agents and users act inside boundaries the system enforces rather than assumes.

Before that: full-stack for fintech and fitness clients, a patient management platform built from scratch for a Dubai clinic, and a CNN-based intrusion detection system that became a peer-reviewed paper. Extraordinary is the only standard worth keeping, and I build so the next generation can build on top.
